Rellora — Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 6, 2026

Overview

Rellora ("the App") is a stress and recovery insights tool developed by VertexLab Solutions LLC. Rellora reads data from Apple Health to help you understand your body's stress and recovery trends in plain language. Your privacy is central to how the App is built. This policy describes how we handle your information.

Health Data We Access

With your explicit permission, Rellora reads the following data from Apple Health (HealthKit) to calculate your daily stress and recovery insights:

  • Heart rate variability (HRV / SDNN)
  • Resting and background heart rate
  • Sleep analysis (stages and duration)
  • Respiratory rate
  • Wrist temperature

Rellora only reads this data — it never writes to, alters, or deletes anything in Apple Health. You control which data types you share, and you can revoke access at any time in the iOS Health app or Settings.

How Your Data Is Processed

All of your health data is processed entirely on your device. Your scores, trends, and personal baselines are computed locally and stored on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework. Your health data is never transmitted to us or to any external server.

AI-Generated Insights

Rellora's plain-English summaries and weekly reports are generated on your device using Apple's on-device Foundation Models framework. Your health data is never sent to any third-party AI provider or cloud service to produce these insights.

How We Use Health Data

Your health data is used solely to provide the App's core functionality — calculating and explaining your stress and recovery trends. In accordance with Apple's requirements, we do not use health data for advertising or marketing, and we do not use it for data mining or share it with any third party for those purposes.

Data Sharing

We do not share, sell, or transfer your personal health data to any third party. Any report or summary you choose to export is generated locally on your device and shared only at your discretion.

Notifications

If you enable reminders or weekly report alerts, the App uses local notifications scheduled on your device. No push notification server receives your health data.

In-App Purchases

Optional subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not collect or have access to your payment information.

Analytics & Tracking

The Rellora app does not use any third-party analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs. We do not track your in-app behavior or share data with advertisers. (This website uses privacy-friendly analytics as described in our site-wide Privacy Policy.)

Data Retention & Deletion

All of your data is stored on your device. Deleting the App removes all associated data. Because there is no server-side copy of your health data, there is nothing for us to delete on our end.

Not a Medical Device

Rellora is a general wellness tool. It does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease or medical condition, and its scores and insights are not medical advice. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional for medical decisions.

Children's Privacy

Rellora is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
