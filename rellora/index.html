Frequently asked

Is my health data private?

Yes. Rellora reads your Apple Health data only with permission, processes everything on your device, and generates its written insights on-device using Apple's Foundation Models. Your health data is never sent to us, to any server, or to any third-party AI.

Do I need an Apple Watch?

An Apple Watch gives the richest data (overnight HRV, sleep, respiratory rate, wrist temperature). You can still use Rellora with iPhone-only heart-rate data, though insights are most accurate with a Watch.

Does it measure cortisol?

No. No wrist wearable can directly measure cortisol. Rellora tracks physiological signals associated with stress and recovery — like HRV, resting heart rate and sleep — and explains their trends. It reports stress and recovery, not hormone levels.

Is Rellora a medical device?

No. Rellora is a general wellness tool. It shows stress and recovery trends and does not diagnose, treat, or measure any medical condition. It is not a substitute for professional medical advice.
