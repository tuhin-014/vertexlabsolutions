CalmNow — Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 3, 2026

Developer: VertexLab Solutions LLC

Overview

CalmNow is a panic and anxiety self-help app for iPhone. It has no account, no server and no analytics. Everything you enter stays on your device. This policy explains exactly what the app stores, where, and how to remove it.

Data Stored on Your Device

We do not collect, receive or store any of your data on our servers. CalmNow has no backend. The following is saved only on your iPhone, using Apple's SwiftData framework and the app's local settings:

  • Journal, worry and thought-record entries you write.
  • Daily check-ins (anxiety ratings) and the history and statistics built from them.
  • Session history for breathing, grounding, body-scan and other exercises.
  • Emergency contacts you type in yourself. CalmNow does not read your Contacts.
  • Onboarding answers and preferences, used to personalise your Calm Plan.

None of this is uploaded, synced to a CalmNow server, or shared with us or with any third party.

Apple Health

If you turn on Apple Health in onboarding or in Settings, CalmNow asks permission to:

  • Write your completed breathing, body-scan and grounding sessions as Mindful Minutes.
  • Read your past Mindful Minutes, to show your relaxation streak.

CalmNow reads and writes no other Health data. Health data stays in Apple Health on your device. It is never sent to us, never used for advertising or marketing, and never shared with third parties. You can revoke access at any time in the Health app → Sharing → Apps → CalmNow. Mindful Minutes that CalmNow has already written stay in Apple Health until you delete them there.

Data We Do NOT Collect

  • No account, name, email or phone number
  • No location data
  • No analytics, crash-reporting or tracking SDKs
  • No advertising identifiers and no ads
  • No microphone or camera access

Calls and Messages

The emergency buttons (for example 911 or 988, or a contact you added) hand the number to your phone's dialer or Messages app. CalmNow does not see or record the call or message.

In-App Purchases

Premium subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store using StoreKit. We never receive your payment details. CalmNow checks your purchase status with Apple on your device. Apple's privacy policy governs these transactions.

Notifications

Reminders you enable are scheduled locally on your device. No notification data is sent to any server.

Export and Deletion

Settings → Export Journal as CSV creates a file that you control and share as you choose. Settings → Clear All Data erases everything CalmNow stores on your device. Deleting the app also removes all of it. Mindful Minutes already written to Apple Health are managed in the Health app.

Not Medical Care

CalmNow is a general wellness app. It is not a medical device and is not a substitute for professional care. If you are in crisis, call or text 988 (US). In an emergency, call your local emergency number.

Children's Privacy

CalmNow is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children. The app collects no personal data from anyone.
