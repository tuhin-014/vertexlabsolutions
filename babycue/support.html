What stays free

BabyCue works without an account and without paying. These are free, and are not part of Pro:

  • Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets — last feed, sleep and diaper at a glance
  • The Apple Watch app — check and log from your wrist
  • Live Activities — the feeding, pumping or sleep timer on your Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island
  • Unlimited logging of feeds, sleep and diapers
  • iCloud backup, so a new phone doesn't cost you your records

Pro adds pattern insights, growth charts, the milestone tracker, a shareable appointment summary, your full history beyond the last 30 days, multiple babies for twins and siblings, and CSV / JSON export.

How do I cancel BabyCue Pro?

Subscriptions are billed by Apple, so you cancel in iOS Settings, not in BabyCue:

  1. Open the Settings app
  2. Tap your name at the top, then Subscriptions
  3. Tap BabyCue, then Cancel Subscription

Cancelling stops future charges. Pro stays on until the end of the period you have already paid for, and nothing is deleted. Every feed, nap and diaper you have logged stays exactly where it is, and the widget, the Watch app and Live Activities keep working.

I already paid — how do I restore my purchase?

Open Settings → Upgrade to Pro inside BabyCue and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure the device is signed in with the Apple Account that made the purchase. If Pro still doesn't unlock, email us with the Apple Account email you used.

Can my partner use the subscription too?

Yes. All three plans support Family Sharing, so everyone in your Apple family group gets Pro on their own devices at no extra cost.

Refunds

Apple handles refunds for App Store purchases, not us. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com. If something in the app didn't work as described, please tell us too so we can fix it.

Widget, Watch and Lock Screen

How do I add the widget?

  1. Touch and hold an empty part of your Home Screen
  2. Tap Edit, then Add Widget
  3. Search for BabyCue and pick a size

Lock Screen widgets work the same way: touch and hold the Lock Screen, tap Customize, then add BabyCue under the clock.

The widget is showing old numbers

iOS decides how often a widget refreshes, so it can lag a little behind the app. Opening BabyCue and returning to the Home Screen refreshes it immediately.

The Apple Watch app is empty

The Watch app is filled in by your iPhone. Open BabyCue on the phone once with the Watch nearby and paired, and the numbers appear. If the Watch has been out of range, anything you logged on it is queued and sent when the two are together again.

The Lock Screen timer

Starting a breastfeeding, pumping or sleep timer puts it on your Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island. You can switch sides or finish the session from there without unlocking the phone — finishing saves the entry for you.

Privacy

Your baby's records stay yours.

There is no account, no sign-in and no ads. What you log is stored on your iPhone and backed up to your own iCloud, in your private database — not to a server of ours. We cannot see it, and nobody else can either. BabyCue does not send us analytics, and there is no third-party tracking in the app.

Because the backup lives in your iCloud rather than with us, we cannot recover your records if you delete the app while signed out of iCloud, or if iCloud is turned off for BabyCue. To keep a copy you control, use Settings → Export Data (CSV / JSON).

Common questions

Can I log a wet and a dirty diaper at once?

Yes — tap Both. It is one entry, not two, which is the whole point at 2am.

Can I track twins?

Multiple babies is a Pro feature. Each baby gets a separate timeline, so feeds and naps never get mixed up between them.

Can I switch to millilitres, kilograms and centimetres?

Yes. Settings → Use Metric (ml, kg, cm).

Does BabyCue tell me when my baby should sleep or feed next?

No, and that is deliberate. BabyCue records what you enter and shows you what actually happened — how long since the last feed, how this week compares with last. It does not predict, schedule or diagnose.

Is BabyCue medical advice?

No. BabyCue is a tracking tool for informational purposes only. Growth and milestone information is a general guideline, and every baby develops at their own pace. Please talk to your pediatrician about any concern, and call your local emergency number if your baby is in distress.

I found a bug, or something is missing

Email us. Real messages from real parents are how this app gets better, and we would rather hear about a rough edge than have you quietly give up on it.
