Settle — the executor's companion

iPhone and iPad · iOS 17+ · free to start

Being named executor is a 12–18 month job nobody trained you for. Settle tells you what to do next — one clear step at a time — from the day you start to the day the estate closes.

Settle is an organisational tool, not a law firm, and does not provide legal advice. It helps you stay organised, on schedule and ready for the court.

What it does

  • A plan built for your state. Answer a few questions and Settle builds a step-by-step plan for your situation, with deadline reminders. Every step explains why it matters and how to do it.
  • It shows its work. Steps backed by law carry the statute and the date we last read it — and anything we have not confirmed at the source says so.
  • Documents where the work is. Photograph the paperwork and it attaches to the step that needs it.
  • The money, tracked. An expense ledger for everything the estate owes you back, and PDF reports for the family or the court.

Get Settle

The plan is free. Unlimited documents, unlimited ledger entries and the PDF reports are the Estate Pass.
