Does an executor have to notify creditors?

Part of Settle's executor guides · 47 jurisdictions · state content checked 2026-09 · page generated 2026-09-28

In most states, yes — and the notice is what starts the clock that eventually lets you distribute safely. Both the method and the window are set by state law, and getting the notice wrong can leave the estate exposed long after you thought it was closed.
