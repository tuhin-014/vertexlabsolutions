Why every figure here has a citation

Most executor advice online is uncited, undated, and often wrong — Florida's summary-administration threshold moved to $150,000 in July 2026 and pages still quote $75,000. So everything on these guides carries the statute it came from and the month we last read it, and anything we recorded from a secondary source without reading the primary text is tagged not yet confirmed rather than quietly presented as checked.
