What goes in an estate inventory?

Part of Settle's executor guides · 51 jurisdictions · state content checked 2026-09 · page generated 2026-09-28

Everything the estate owned at the date of death, valued as of that date — real property, accounts, vehicles, personal property of real value, and any business interest. Whether you have to file it with the court, and by when, is the part that differs by state.

See also: Estate inventory deadlines by state
