Estate inventory deadlines by state

29 states · generated 2026-09-27 from the same registry the app ships

Most states require the executor to file an inventory of everything the estate owns, valued as of the date of death, within a fixed window after appointment. It is the single most common step to miss, because nothing prompts you — the court simply expects it.

How to check us

Every figure on this page links to the statute or court source it came from and shows the date we last read it. 22 of the 29 citations on this page carry the “not yet confirmed” tag. That means we recorded the citation from a secondary source and have not yet read it against the primary statute text ourselves. We could have left those rows out and the page would look better — but it would imply the rest were checked too, which is exactly the impression nobody should be given about a court deadline.

The full registry, including everything we have not confirmed, is published at Where the state information comes from. If the court or your attorney gave you a different date, please tell us at support@vertexlabsolutions.com — it is the fastest way to get it fixed for the next executor.
