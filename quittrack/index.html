❓ FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about QuitTrack

What is the best quit smoking app?

QuitTrack is one of the best quit smoking apps available for iPhone. It combines streak tracking, a real-time money saved calculator, a health recovery timeline, and shareable milestone cards to keep you motivated. It works for smoking, vaping, or any other bad habit you want to quit. Download it free from the App Store.

Can QuitTrack help me quit other bad habits besides smoking?

Yes. QuitTrack is designed for quitting any bad habit — smoking, vaping, drinking, gambling, junk food, social media overuse, or any other behavior you want to stop. Simply name your habit, set your quit date, and start building your streak.

How does QuitTrack calculate money saved?

During setup, you enter how much you typically spend on your habit per day or per week. QuitTrack multiplies that by the exact time elapsed since your quit date and displays your real-time savings. Watching that number grow is one of the most powerful motivators to keep going.

Is QuitTrack free to download?

Yes, QuitTrack is free to download from the App Store. Core features including habit tracking, streak counting, money saved calculator, health timeline, and milestone cards are available at no cost.

What happens when I reach a milestone in QuitTrack?

QuitTrack celebrates your milestones — 24 hours, 1 week, 1 month, 6 months, 1 year — with beautifully designed shareable milestone cards. You can post these to Instagram, send them via Messages, or share them anywhere to show your progress and inspire others.

Does QuitTrack track health recovery after quitting smoking?

Yes. QuitTrack includes a health timeline that shows the measurable health improvements happening in your body — from just 20 minutes after quitting to 10 years down the road. Seeing your body recover in real time is a powerful reminder of why you quit.

What if I slip up? Does QuitTrack help you start over?

Slipping up is part of the quit journey for many people. QuitTrack lets you reset your streak and start fresh without judgment. Every quit attempt brings you closer to success — the app keeps your history so you can see how far you've come overall.
