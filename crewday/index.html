Frequently asked

My GC already makes me use their system. Why would I need this?

That's exactly why. Reports you submit into the GC's platform live in the GC's account — remember the sub who couldn't prove he'd ever submitted them. CrewDay works alongside whatever the GC mandates: you send the same PDF, and your own timestamped copy stays with you.

I already take photos of everything. Isn't that enough?

Photos prove what things looked like — they don't record manpower, hours, deliveries, or why the schedule slipped. A photo of a flooded slab doesn't say who was on site, what got done anyway, and how many hours were lost. The report does. CrewDay attaches your photos to it automatically.

What will it cost?

One flat price for your whole crew — planned at $9.99/month or $79.99/year, with a free tier so you can try it on a real job first. No per-user seats, no per-report fees.

Is it iOS only?

iPhone first — that's where we can make it fastest and most reliable, fully offline. Android is planned next.
