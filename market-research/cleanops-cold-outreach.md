## Sending Guidelines

- **Volume:** 50 emails per week (10/day, Mon-Fri)
- **Timing:** Send between 7-9 AM local time (before their first job)
- **Personalization:** Always use real business name and city -- never send generic
- **Reply handling:** Any reply gets a personal response within 2 hours
- **Unsubscribe:** Honor all opt-outs immediately
- **CAN-SPAM compliance:** Include physical address and unsubscribe option in all emails

## Lead Sources

1. Google Maps / Google Places API -- search "cleaning service" in target cities
2. Yelp listings for cleaning services
3. Facebook business pages in cleaning groups
4. Instagram hashtag searches (#cleaningbusiness, #maidservice)
