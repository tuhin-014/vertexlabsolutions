Cancel a subscription or free trial

This is the most common reason people write to us, so it's first. Apple bills every subscription and free trial, not us, which means we're not able to cancel one for you — but you can do it yourself in about twenty seconds:

  1. Open the Settings app on your iPhone or iPad
  2. Tap your name at the very top
  3. Tap Subscriptions
  4. Tap the app you want to cancel
  5. Tap Cancel Subscription, then confirm

Cancel before the trial ends and you are not charged anything. You keep access until the last day of the trial or the period you've already paid for, and nothing on your device is deleted.

If you don't see the app listed under Subscriptions, it was either already cancelled or bought with a different Apple Account — check that you're signed in with the one used to subscribe.

Refunds

Apple handles all App Store refunds, so we can't issue one directly. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com: sign in with your Apple Account, find the charge, and choose Request a refund. Apple decides these, usually within a couple of days.

If something didn't work as described, please tell us as well — a refund fixes your bill, but only a bug report fixes the app.

Restore a purchase

If you've paid before and the app is asking you to pay again, look for Restore Purchases on the app's subscribe or settings screen. Make sure the device is signed in with the Apple Account that originally paid. If it still doesn't unlock, email us with that Apple Account address and we'll look into it.
