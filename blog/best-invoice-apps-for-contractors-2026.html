If you are a plumber, electrician, HVAC tech, or general contractor, you already know that getting paid quickly depends on one thing: getting the invoice out fast. The longer you wait after a job, the longer your client waits to pay, and the longer your cash flow suffers.

The best invoice apps for contractors in 2026 let you create and send professional invoices directly from the job site, often in under two minutes. No more paper invoices, no more driving back to the office, and no more forgotten line items.

We tested seven of the top contractor invoice apps to help you find the right fit for your trade and business size.

What Makes a Good Contractor Invoice App?

Generic invoicing software is built for accountants, not tradespeople. Here is what separates a contractor-grade invoice app from a one-size-fits-all billing tool:

  • Fast on-site entry -- You should be able to create an invoice before leaving the driveway. If it takes more than two minutes, you will not do it consistently.
  • Trade-specific templates -- A plumber's invoice looks different from a general contractor's. Pre-built templates with the right line items save setup time and reduce errors.
  • Offline capability -- Cell signal is unreliable in basements, rural areas, and large job sites. Your app must work without internet and sync when you reconnect.
  • Voice input -- Typing on a phone with dirty gloves is painful. Voice dictation for line items and notes is a game-changer on the job site.
  • Professional PDF output -- Your invoice is part of your brand. It should look polished when the client opens it, not like a spreadsheet export.
  • No steep learning curve -- Most contractors are not accountants. The app should be useful from day one without a training course.

The 7 Best Invoice Apps for Contractors in 2026

TradeInvoice

TradeInvoice is the only invoice app built from scratch for tradespeople on iPhone. Most contractor invoicing apps started as general accounting software and bolted on a mobile app later. TradeInvoice was designed for the job site from day one, which shows in every detail.

The app runs fully offline, so invoices created in a basement or remote job site sync automatically when you get back in range.

2
Jobber
Best for: Growing service businesses that need full job management

Jobber is the most popular all-in-one field service management platform for contractors. It goes well beyond invoicing to cover job scheduling, client management, quote-to-invoice workflows, and online payment collection. If you run a team and need to coordinate multiple technicians across multiple jobs per day, Jobber provides the operational infrastructure to do it.

The invoicing feature is solid: create invoices from completed job cards, include photos from the job site, and send automated payment reminders. However, Jobber is significantly more expensive than a dedicated invoicing app, and the full feature set can feel overwhelming for a solo contractor who just needs to get invoices out the door.

  • Complete job management from quote to payment
  • GPS tracking and scheduling for field teams
  • Client portal with online payment
  • Integrates with QuickBooks
3
Invoice Ninja
Best for: Budget-conscious contractors who need a free desktop option

Invoice Ninja is a popular open-source invoicing tool with a free tier that handles unlimited clients and invoices. It is more powerful than most free alternatives and includes recurring invoices, expense tracking, time tracking, and a client portal where customers can view and pay invoices online.

The mobile app is functional but not as polished as dedicated contractor tools. If you primarily invoice from a desktop or laptop after the job, Invoice Ninja is excellent value. If you need a great on-site mobile experience, the trade-off in UX compared to mobile-first apps like TradeInvoice becomes apparent quickly.

  • Generous free tier with unlimited invoices
  • Recurring invoices and automatic payment reminders
  • Client portal with online payment options
  • Self-hosted option for privacy-conscious users
4
FreshBooks
Best for: Contractors who need accounting alongside invoicing

FreshBooks started as an invoicing tool and has grown into a full small business accounting platform. For contractors who want their invoicing, expense tracking, mileage logging, and tax preparation all in one place, FreshBooks is one of the cleaner options compared to QuickBooks.

The mobile app is well-designed, and the invoices look professional. The main downside is pricing: FreshBooks costs between $19 and $55 per month depending on the plan, which is hard to justify for a solo contractor who primarily needs fast on-site invoicing. There is no trade-specific customization, and the app is not designed for offline-first use.

  • Clean, professional invoice templates
  • Built-in accounting and expense tracking
  • Time tracking and project profitability reporting
  • Automated late payment reminders
5
Housecall Pro
Best for: HVAC, plumbing, and electrical service businesses with multiple techs

Housecall Pro is purpose-built for home service contractors, which gives it a meaningful advantage over general invoicing tools. The app understands trade workflows: you can create service agreements, sell maintenance plans, dispatch technicians with GPS, and collect payment on-site. The invoicing and payment collection tools are tightly integrated with the service job flow.

Like Jobber, Housecall Pro is priced for businesses rather than individual tradespeople, starting at around $69 per month. It is genuinely excellent for an HVAC or plumbing company with a team of technicians, but overkill and expensive for a solo operator who just needs to send invoices from the van.

  • Trade-specific dispatch and job management
  • On-site payment collection with card readers
  • Service agreement and maintenance plan management
  • Customer texting and automated review requests
6
Square Invoices
Best for: Contractors who already use Square for payment processing

Square Invoices is free to use, and if you already accept payments through Square, it is a natural fit. You can create and send invoices, accept credit card payments with no monthly fee, set up recurring invoices, and enable tipping -- useful for residential service work. The Square ecosystem also handles in-person payment via tap-to-pay on iPhone.

The invoice templates are clean but generic. There is no trade-specific customization, no voice input, and the mobile experience, while decent, is not optimized for the job site. Square Invoices works best as part of a broader Square payment workflow rather than as a standalone contractor invoicing solution.

  • Free to use -- pay only transaction fees when clients pay
  • Seamless integration with Square card readers
  • Tap-to-pay support on newer iPhones
  • Recurring invoices and auto-reminders
7
QuickBooks Simple Start
Best for: Contractors who need full accounting and tax prep

QuickBooks is the accounting industry standard, and its Simple Start plan includes invoicing, expense tracking, mileage logging, and basic reporting at around $18 per month. For contractors who work with an accountant at tax time, having your books already in QuickBooks saves hours of reconciliation work every year.

As an invoicing experience, QuickBooks is functional but not delightful. The mobile app is reliable, but it was designed for accountants first and tradespeople second. If you want a QuickBooks integration without the full platform cost, many of the other apps on this list sync with QuickBooks directly -- including field service tools like Jobber and Housecall Pro.

  • Industry-standard accounting trusted by bookkeepers and CPAs
  • Mileage tracking and expense categorization
  • Tax-ready reports that work with any accountant
  • Integrates with hundreds of contractor tools

How to Choose the Right Invoice App for Your Trade

The best invoice app depends on how and where you work. Here is a simple decision guide:

  • Solo contractor on the road all day? Use TradeInvoice -- it is free, fast, and built for the job site with voice input and offline support.
  • Running a team of 3+ techs? Look at Jobber or Housecall Pro for full dispatch and job management.
  • Already using Square for payments? Square Invoices is a natural, free addition to your workflow.
  • Working with a CPA or bookkeeper? QuickBooks Simple Start keeps your books tax-ready year-round.
  • Want invoicing and accounting without a big monthly fee? FreshBooks Lite or Invoice Ninja offer a middle ground.

The hidden cost of delayed invoicing: Research consistently shows that invoices sent within two hours of job completion are paid 45% faster than invoices sent the following day. Over a year, that cash-flow difference can mean tens of thousands of dollars sitting in client accounts instead of yours. The right app removes every barrier between job completion and invoice sent.

Tips for Getting Paid Faster as a Contractor

Even the best invoice app is only part of the equation. Here are four practices that consistently speed up payment:

1. Invoice on-site, not at the office

The moment you finish a job, open your invoice app and create the invoice while the client is still there. Reviewing it together on the spot removes the "I never got the invoice" excuse and often prompts immediate payment.

2. Include photos from the job

Attaching before-and-after photos to your invoice documents the work and reduces disputes. It also looks professional and builds client trust. Most modern invoice apps let you attach photos directly to the invoice.

3. Set clear payment terms upfront

State your payment terms on every invoice: "Net 7" or "Due upon receipt" leaves no ambiguity. For larger jobs, include a deposit requirement in your quote so you are not carrying the full cost of materials yourself.

4. Accept multiple payment methods

Every payment barrier is a delay. Accept credit cards, bank transfer, and digital wallets so clients can pay the moment they decide to. Cash and check-only policies cost you time and, increasingly, clients.

Final Thoughts

The best invoice app for most contractors is the one that removes every possible obstacle between finishing the job and getting paid. For solo tradespeople and small crews, that means a mobile-first app with fast entry, offline capability, and professional output -- without a monthly subscription eating into your margin.

If you are a plumber, electrician, HVAC tech, or any kind of field service contractor on iPhone, we recommend starting with TradeInvoice. It is free, it works offline, and the voice input alone will change how you handle paperwork on the job site. For businesses that have grown to multiple technicians and need scheduling and dispatch, Jobber or Housecall Pro are worth the investment.
