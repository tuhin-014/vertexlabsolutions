2. Description of Service

SuppSage is a supplement scanner and companion. It identifies supplements from a barcode or label, calculates an educational quality score (SageScore), summarizes ingredients and combined intake, flags possible interactions and FDA recalls, and lets you optionally track how you feel over time. SuppSage is an informational tool, not a medical device or diagnostic service.
