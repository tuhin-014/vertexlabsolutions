SuppSage — Supplement Scanner

Last updated: July 17, 2026

Overview

SuppSage ("the App") is developed by VertexLab Solutions LLC. We built SuppSage to be private by design. This policy explains how we handle your data.

What stays on your device

Your supplement stack, the products you scan, your intake, any "effects" check-ins you log, and your optional profile (age and sex) are stored locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData and system storage. This information is not sent to our servers, is not sold, and is not used for advertising. We do not have access to it.

What is sent to our service, and when

To identify a scanned product and calculate its SageScore, the App sends the scanned barcode, or a photo of the label (only if you choose "add by photo"), to our backend service. To check for recalls, the App sends the brand names of the products in your stack. These requests are transmitted over an encrypted connection and are used only to return product information, scores, and recall matches. We do not store your stack on our servers; brand and product queries are cached transiently to improve performance.

Data sources & providers

Product and safety information comes from public sources including the NIH Dietary Supplement Label Database (DSLD), the NIH Office of Dietary Supplements, and U.S. FDA enforcement (recall) reports. Our backend proxy is hosted on Cloudflare, Inc. The optional photo-label reader is powered by Anthropic, PBC (the Claude AI model); if you use it, the label photo is processed to extract the printed ingredients and is not used to train AI models, sold, or retained beyond processing your request. If you never use the photo reader, no photos leave your device.

Notifications

With your permission, SuppSage sends local notifications to alert you if a product in your stack matches an FDA recall. These are generated on your device. You can turn them off any time in iOS Settings.

Analytics & tracking

SuppSage does not use advertising SDKs, tracking pixels, or cross-app tracking, and does not track your behavior across apps or websites. We keep anonymous, aggregate counts of events such as "a scan happened" to understand reliability; these contain no personal information and are not tied to you or your device.

Third-party services

In-app purchases are handled by Apple's StoreKit 2 and are subject to Apple's privacy policy. We never see your payment details.

Children's privacy

SuppSage is not intended for children under 17. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Health & medical disclaimer

SuppSage provides educational information about dietary supplements and is not medical advice. Scores are based on public data, not laboratory testing. See our full Medical Disclaimer, and always consult a healthcare professional before changing what you take.

Your choices

You can delete any product from your stack, and removing the App from your device deletes all locally stored data. To request anything else, contact us below.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
