Privacy Policy — FocusPin

Last updated: October 24, 2026

Developer: VertexLab Solutions LLC

Overview

FocusPin ("the App") is a one-task focus app with a habit tracker, body-doubling rooms and ambient sound, built for ADHD brains. This policy explains what the App does with your data. The short version: your tasks, habits and sessions never leave your device.

Data stored on your device

  • Tasks, habits, focus sessions, brain dumps, streaks and settings — stored locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData. We have no server that receives them and no way to read them.
  • No accounts or sign-in — the App never asks you to create an account.
  • No analytics, no advertising and no tracking — the App contains no third-party analytics or advertising SDKs and does not track you across apps or websites.
  • Smart Split and task suggestions run entirely on-device. No task text is sent to any AI service.

Focus Together (body-doubling rooms)

If you choose to join a solo, public or private room, the App contacts our presence service at presence.vertexlabsolutions.com so that you can see how many people are focusing alongside you. The App sends only a random session identifier generated on your device and the room you joined; it never sends your task names, habits, notes or any account information. The service keeps no persistent record: a session disappears about a minute after your last heartbeat, and nothing about your focusing history is stored. Solo mode makes no network requests at all.

Microphone and speech recognition

If you use voice task entry, the App asks for microphone and speech-recognition permission. Audio is processed by Apple's speech-recognition framework to turn your words into a task title; the App does not record, store or transmit audio. You can decline these permissions and type tasks instead.

Notifications, widgets and Apple Watch

The App may ask permission to show local notifications for session and habit reminders. Reminders, Lock Screen and Home Screen widgets and the Apple Watch companion all read the same on-device data; nothing is sent to us.

Purchases

If the App offers optional in-app purchases, they are processed by Apple. We receive no payment details. Purchase and subscription data is governed by Apple's privacy policy.

Deleting your data

Settings → "Clear All Data" erases every task, habit, session and setting from the device. Deleting the App removes everything it stored. Because we hold no copy, there is nothing further for us to delete.

Children's privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

Not medical advice

FocusPin is a productivity tool. It does not diagnose, treat or provide medical advice for ADHD or any other condition.

Changes

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
