DOT Compliance Desk

Privacy Policy

DOT Compliance Desk by VertexLab Solutions LLC · Last updated September 27, 2026

DOT Compliance Desk helps motor carriers keep their driver, vehicle and company records in order. To do that we hold records about the carrier and about the people who drive for it. This page says what we hold, why, who else sees it, and how long we keep it.

Who this covers

  • Carriers: the company that signs up, and the person who uses the app for it.
  • Drivers: people whose records a carrier sends us. The carrier is our customer, but the records are about you, and the section "If you are a driver" below is for you.

The service is offered in the United States only.

What we collect

  • Account details: your mobile number, which we use to text you a sign-in code, and your reminder settings.
  • Company details: legal name, USDOT and MC numbers, base state, and the answers you give during setup about how you operate. We also read your carrier's public record from the Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration (FMCSA).
  • Driver records: name, mobile number, license details, hire and last-day dates, and the documents in a driver qualification file. These include medical examiner's certificates, driving records, Clearinghouse query records, and drug and alcohol testing program enrollment.
  • Vehicle records: unit number, vehicle details and inspection documents.
  • Documents and photos: the pictures and files you or your drivers send us, by the app or by text.
  • Messages: what you write to the Desk, the texts between us and you or your drivers, and any voicemail you leave. We do not record phone calls. Voicemail is recorded, and the greeting says so before the beep.
  • Consent and opt-out records: the carrier's confirmation that a driver agreed to receive texts, and anyone who has replied STOP.
  • Plan status: which plan you have and when it renews, from the signed receipt Apple gives us. Apple takes the payment. We never see your card or bank details.
  • A log of actions: who viewed or changed a record and when, so that access to driver records can be checked.

What we do not collect

  • We do not track your location.
  • The app contains no advertising software, no third-party analytics and no trackers.
  • Face ID is handled by your iPhone. We never receive your face data; the app is only told whether the unlock succeeded.

Permissions the app asks for

  • Camera: to photograph driver and vehicle documents.
  • Photo library: to bring in a document you have already photographed.
  • Face ID: to keep records private if someone else picks up your phone.
  • Notifications: for deadline reminders.

Why we use it

  • To keep the carrier's compliance records and show what is due, missing or expiring.
  • To send reminders, and to ask drivers for the documents only they can provide.
  • To prepare filings and record-review requests, which are sent only after the carrier approves them.
  • To answer questions sent to the Desk.
  • To confirm a plan is active.

A driver's records are used only to keep the records of the carrier that sent them. We do not sell personal information. We do not combine drivers' records across carriers, and we do not build driver scores or profiles.

Who sees it

  • Our compliance specialists, who review the documents and prepare filings.
  • Twilio, which delivers our text messages and carries calls and voicemail.
  • Apple, which processes plan payments and tells us the result.
  • Government agencies such as FMCSA and state agencies, when the carrier approves a filing or a record-review request and we submit it for them.
  • Our hosting providers, which store the data on our behalf.
  • Others when the law requires it, such as a valid subpoena or court order.

Text messages

We text carriers about deadlines and sign-in codes, and we text drivers only after the carrier has asked us to and has confirmed that the driver agreed to be texted. We keep a record of that confirmation. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to any message to end all texts from us, or HELP for help. Replying STOP also ends sign-in codes, so carriers who only want fewer reminders should use the switch in the app instead. We do not share mobile numbers or consent records with anyone for marketing.

How long we keep it

Federal rules require carriers to keep many of these records for set periods. A driver qualification file, for example, must be kept for three years after the driver's last day (49 CFR 391.51). For that reason the app does not delete a driver's file when they leave; it records their last day and keeps the file for the required period.

When a carrier closes its account, we delete its records on request unless a law or a legal hold requires us to keep them. To ask, email us at the address below.

If you are a driver

You can ask to see the records we hold about you and ask for a mistake to be corrected. Email us at the address below and tell us which carrier you drive or drove for. We may need to confirm who you are before we answer. Some records belong to the carrier's required file and can be corrected but not removed while the law requires the carrier to keep them.

Your choices

  • Turn deadline texts on or off in the app.
  • Reply STOP to end texts.
  • Turn camera, photo, Face ID and notification access on or off in your iPhone's Settings.
  • Ask us for a copy of your records, a correction, or deletion of your account.
  • Manage or cancel a plan in your Apple account settings.

Depending on the state you live in, you may have further rights over your personal information, including the right to appeal a decision we make about your request. We honor those rights for everyone, wherever you live in the United States.

Security

Connections between the app and our servers are encrypted. The app can be locked with Face ID. Access to driver records by our staff requires two-step sign-in and is logged. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects your information we will tell you as the law requires.

Children

DOT Compliance Desk is a business tool for adults. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 13.

DOT Compliance Desk provides compliance-management tools and administrative assistance based on information you supply and on publicly available regulatory information. We are not a law firm, an accounting firm, a government agency, or a certified medical examiner, and we do not provide legal, tax, or medical advice. Requirements change and vary by jurisdiction and type of operation. You remain solely responsible for your company's regulatory compliance.
