Privacy Policy

Effective: 2026-05-10 · Operator: VertexLab Solutions LLC.

Plain-language summary

  • Anatomly stores your study progress and preferences on your device.
  • We do not track you for advertising. We do not sell or share your data.
  • If you use AI features, the question text + your choice are sent to Anthropic to generate a response. No name, email, or identifier is sent.
  • If you sign in with Apple, we use it only to sync your progress between your devices.
  • Spotter Battle uses a random session ID — no personal data exchanged with opponents.

DataWhyWhere it lives

What we do NOT collect

  • Name, email, phone, address (unless you contact support)
  • Location · Contacts · Photos · Health data
  • Advertising identifiers — we don't run ads and don't track across apps
  • Payment information (handled entirely by Apple StoreKit)

Third parties

  • Anthropic — receives anonymized question text + your choice when AI features are used. See anthropic.com/legal/privacy.
  • Apple — handles all payment + in-app purchase processing.
  • Cloudflare — hosts the Spotter Battle matchmaking server. No personal data is logged.

Children

Anatomly is rated 12+ and intended for medical, nursing, and allied health students. It is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Your rights

  • Erase your data: Tap Profile → Erase All Data.
  • Export your data: Email support@vertexlabsolutions.com.

If you are in California (CCPA) or the EU (GDPR), the rights above satisfy your access, deletion, and portability rights.
