Privacy Policy — Mobile Mechanic Pro

Last updated: April 16, 2026

Developer: VertexLab Solutions LLC

Overview

Mobile Mechanic Pro ("the App") is a job management application for mobile mechanics on iOS. Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains what data the App collects and how it is used.

Data Collection

Mobile Mechanic Pro stores all data locally on your device. We do not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on external servers. Specifically:

  • All business data (clients, vehicles, jobs, invoices) is stored in a local SQLite database on your device.
  • Photos taken within the App are stored locally on your device and are never uploaded.
  • No analytics, crash reporting, or tracking SDKs are included.
  • No personal information is collected, processed, or shared.

Permissions

The App may request the following device permissions:

  • Camera — To take before/after photos of work performed on vehicles.
  • Photo Library — To attach existing photos to job records.

These permissions operate entirely on-device. Photos are stored locally and are never uploaded or transmitted.

In-App Purchases

Mobile Mechanic Pro offers optional auto-renewable subscriptions and a one-time lifetime purchase to unlock Pro features. Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not have access to your payment information. Subscription management and cancellation are handled through your Apple ID settings.

Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, tracking, or data collection services.

Data Sharing

We do not share any data with any third party. There is no data to share because no data is collected.

Data Deletion

Since all data is stored locally on your device, you can delete it at any time by using the "Reset All Data" option in Settings, clearing the App's data in your device settings, or by uninstalling the App.

Children's Privacy

Mobile Mechanic Pro does not collect data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy is updated, the revised version will be posted at this URL with a new effective date.
