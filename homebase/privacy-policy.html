HomeBase — Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 28, 2026

Developer: VertexLab Solutions LLC

Overview

HomeBase is a home inventory and maintenance app for iPhone and iPad. It has no account, no server of its own and no analytics. Everything you enter stays on your device. This policy explains exactly what the app stores, the one feature that contacts a third-party service, and how to remove your data.

Data Stored on Your Device

We do not collect, receive or store any of your data on our servers. HomeBase has no backend. The following is saved only on your device, using Apple's SwiftData framework:

  • Your home and rooms (name, address, room names and an optional photo).
  • Inventory items: name, brand, model and serial numbers, barcode, category, purchase date and price, warranty expiry, notes and photos.
  • Maintenance tasks and their schedules.
  • Expenses and any receipt photos you attach.

None of this is uploaded, synced to a HomeBase server, or shared with us or with any third party. HomeBase does not offer cloud backup; your data is included in your normal iPhone or iCloud device backup only if you have that turned on in iOS.

Barcode Lookup (HomeBase Pro)

When you scan a product barcode, HomeBase sends only the barcode number to the UPC Item Database (upcitemdb.com) to look up the product's name, brand and model. No personal information, no photo and nothing else from your inventory is sent. The request is governed by that service's privacy policy. If the lookup fails or you are offline, you can fill in the item details yourself.

Camera and Photos

HomeBase asks for camera access to scan barcodes and to photograph your belongings, and for photo library access to attach existing photos to items and receipts. Photos are stored locally within the app. HomeBase does not read any other photos in your library.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, HomeBase schedules maintenance and warranty reminders locally on your device. No notification data leaves your device.

Data We Do NOT Collect

  • No account, name, email or phone number
  • No location data
  • No analytics, crash-reporting or tracking SDKs
  • No advertising identifiers and no ads
  • No contacts, microphone or health data

Exports

CSV exports and the insurance inventory report are files generated on your device and handed to the iOS share sheet. Where they go after that is your choice; HomeBase never receives a copy.

In-App Purchases

HomeBase Pro subscriptions are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store using StoreKit. We never receive your payment details. HomeBase checks your subscription status with Apple on your device. Apple's privacy policy governs these transactions.

Data Deletion

Settings → Data → Clear All Data permanently deletes every home, room, item, task, expense and photo and cancels all scheduled reminders. Deleting the app removes everything as well. Because nothing is stored on our servers, there is nothing further for us to delete.

Children's Privacy

HomeBase is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.
