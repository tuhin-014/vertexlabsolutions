Common questions

Is my data private? Yes. No bank linking, no data collection. Everything stays on your device and your private iCloud. See the Privacy Policy.

How do I manage or cancel BudgetQuest Pro? Settings → your Apple ID → Subscriptions. Cancelling never locks your data — the free features and all your records stay available.

How do I export my data? In the app: Profile → Data → Export as CSV.

I was using BudgetQuest before Pro existed. You're a Founding Player — everything you already used stays free for you, forever.
