StepMosaic — Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 22, 2026

Developer: VertexLab Solutions LLC

Overview

StepMosaic turns a learning goal into short activities you can run with a child and log in seconds. It is used by families and by special education professionals.

It has no account, no server of its own, and no analytics. The app makes no network requests carrying your information — there is no backend for it to call. Everything you record about a child stays on the device you recorded it on. This policy explains exactly what is stored, what the app asks permission for, and what leaves the device only when you choose to send it.

Data Stored on Your Device

We do not collect, receive or store any of your data. The following is saved only on your device, using Apple's SwiftData framework:

  • Child profiles: a display name or nickname, an approximate age band, and the goal areas you are working on.
  • Goals, chosen from the built-in library or typed in yourself, including text you paste from an IEP.
  • Sessions: which activity was run, roughly how it went, how long it took, and any note you add. Where a goal is set to count trials, the score you enter.
  • Visual schedules, their steps, and any photos you attach to them.
  • Work samples: photos or scans of something the child made, with the date.
  • Accommodations you have ticked, and your app settings.

Deliberate Limits on What the App Will Hold

Some things are not stored because the app does not ask for them and provides nowhere to put them:

  • No date of birth. The app asks for an approximate age band instead. A date of birth is a far stronger identifier and is not needed to choose an activity.
  • No diagnosis field. There is nowhere in StepMosaic to record a diagnosis, and there will not be.
  • No surname. A first name, a nickname or initials is all the app asks for, and that is what professionals are directed to use.
  • No medical or clinical records. StepMosaic is a practice log, not an assessment and not a clinical measure.

Text You Paste From an IEP

If you paste goal text from an IEP, that text is matched against the built-in activity library on your device and stored there. It is never uploaded, never sent to any third party, and never used to train anything. It is the most sensitive field in the app and it is treated that way.

Photos, Camera and Document Scanning

When you add a photo to a schedule step or a work sample, StepMosaic opens Apple's photo picker, camera, or document scanner. The picker hands the app only the images you choose; the app does not read your photo library. Captured images are stored inside the app's own storage on the device.

Notifications

If you turn on a daily reminder, it is scheduled locally on your device. There are no push notifications, because there is no server to send them. Nothing about reminders leaves the device.

The Home Screen and Lock Screen Widget

The widget reads a small summary of today's activities from a private container shared between the app and the widget on the same device. It never leaves the device. Because a Lock Screen can be read by anyone nearby, the widget never shows a child's name on the Lock Screen, and Settings has an option to hide names on the Home Screen widget too.

Data We Do Not Collect

  • No account, email address, phone number or password
  • No analytics, crash-reporting, attribution or tracking SDKs
  • No advertising identifiers and no ads
  • No location, contacts, microphone or health data
  • No artificial-intelligence features, and so no data sent to any model

Exports and Sharing

Progress reports, data sheets, printable picture cards and the CSV export are files generated on your device and handed to the iOS share sheet. Where a file goes after that is entirely your choice, and we never receive a copy. CSV export remains available whether or not you subscribe: a record of your own child is yours.

There is no connection between a family's copy of the app and a professional's copy. Nothing is shared automatically, there is no teacher or parent account, and a profile created by a parent is never treated as authorising anyone else to see it.

Use by Schools and Professionals

StepMosaic is a personal practice tool. It is not a student information system, it does not store education records, and it should not be used as a system of record for anything your school or district holds. Professionals are asked to enter first names or initials only. If your employer requires an approved vendor or a data agreement before you may record information about a student, follow that requirement — this app does not replace it.

In-App Purchases

StepMosaic Plus is sold through Apple using StoreKit. Payment is handled entirely by Apple; we never see your payment details. The app checks your purchase status with Apple on your device. Apple's privacy policy governs those transactions.

Children's Privacy

StepMosaic is a tool for adults — a parent, carer, teacher or therapist — to record their work with a child. It is not designed for a child to use on their own, and it is not submitted to the Kids Category.

Information about a child is entered by that adult and stays on their device. Because the app has no server, no account and no analytics, there is no online collection of a child's information by us at any point, and nothing for us to disclose, sell or share. We do not knowingly receive information from or about anyone, of any age, because we do not receive information at all.

Data Deletion

Settings lets you delete a single child or delete everything. Deletion is immediate, local and irreversible. Deleting the app removes everything too. Because nothing is stored on our servers, there is nothing further for us to delete and no request to make.
