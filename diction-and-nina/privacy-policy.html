Quest for the Golden Pages — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 10, 2026

VertexLab Solutions ("we", "our", or "us") makes Quest for the Golden Pages. This app is made for children, and it is built so that there is nothing about a child to collect. This page says exactly what that means.

The short version

We collect nothing. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party SDKs of any kind. Nothing a child does in this app is sent to us, and we have no way of knowing that any particular person is using it.

Information stored on the device

The app keeps its own state in the app's private storage on the device — for the game, that is the save file: progress, collected items, settings, and which characters have been unlocked. For the controller, nothing is kept at all beyond the current session. This data never leaves the device, is not backed up to our servers (we have none), and is deleted when the app is deleted.

Playing together over the internet

The game has an optional online mode in which two people who already know each other can play together by sharing a short room code, and the companion controller app connects to a game the same way. When and only when somebody chooses to use it:

  • The two devices connect directly to each other using WebRTC. Game moves travel between the two devices and do not pass through us.
  • To make that direct connection, each device asks a public STUN server for its own public IP address, and exchanges that address with the other device. This is how every video call on the internet works. If a direct connection cannot be made, an optional TURN relay may forward the encrypted connection instead.
  • The room code is typed by the players and shared by them, out loud or by message. We never see it.
  • No names, no messages, no accounts, and no personal details are exchanged — only the state of the game.

If online play is never used, the app makes no network connections at all. It is fully playable offline.

Children's privacy

This app is intended for children and complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). We do not knowingly collect, and in fact have no mechanism to collect, personal information from anyone of any age. There is no user-generated content shared with strangers, no chat with strangers, no social features, no advertising, no in-app purchases, and no links out of the app to other websites.

Data we share

None. We have no servers that receive data from this app, so there is nothing to share, sell, or disclose — to anyone, for any purpose, ever.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date at the top. If a change ever meant we started collecting something, we would say so plainly and prominently.
