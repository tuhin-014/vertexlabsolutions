Program Name

ContractorFlow SMS Notifications

Program Description

ContractorFlow sends automated SMS messages on behalf of local contractors and service professionals to their customers. Messages include appointment confirmations, scheduling reminders, missed call auto-replies, invoice payment reminders, and service review requests.

Message Frequency

Message frequency varies based on your interactions with your contractor. You may receive messages for:

  • Appointment booking confirmations (1 per booking)
  • Appointment reminders (1 per upcoming appointment)
  • Missed call auto-replies (1 per missed call)
  • Invoice payment reminders (up to 3 per invoice)
  • Service review requests (1 per completed job)

Typical frequency: 1-5 messages per month depending on service activity.

Message and Data Rates

Message and data rates may apply. Check with your mobile carrier for details about your messaging plan.

Opt-In

By providing your phone number when booking an appointment, calling or texting a contractor's business number, or being added as a customer, you consent to receive transactional SMS messages from ContractorFlow on behalf of your contractor.

Opt-Out

To stop receiving messages, reply STOP to any message. You will receive a confirmation message and no further messages will be sent. You can also contact your contractor directly to be removed.

Help
