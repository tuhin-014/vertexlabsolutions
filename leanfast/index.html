❓ FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about LeanFast

What is the best intermittent fasting app?

LeanFast is one of the best intermittent fasting apps for iPhone. It combines a smart fasting timer, detailed progress charts, daily reminders, and an AI coach in one free app. With support for 16:8, OMAD, 18:6, 20:4, 5:2, and fully custom fasting plans, LeanFast adapts to your schedule and goals.

Does LeanFast work as a fasting timer app?

Yes. LeanFast includes a full-featured fasting timer that counts down your fasting window in real time. You receive notifications when your fast starts and ends, plus a live countdown so you always know exactly where you are in your fasting cycle.

Can I use LeanFast as a 16:8 fasting tracker?

Absolutely. LeanFast comes with built-in support for the 16:8 fasting schedule. Select the 16:8 plan during setup and the app automatically configures your fasting and eating windows, sends smart reminders, and tracks your daily streaks and consistency.

Is LeanFast free to download?

Yes, LeanFast is free to download from the App Store. Core features including the fasting timer, preset plans, progress charts, and daily reminders are available at no cost.

What fasting schedules does LeanFast support?

LeanFast supports 16:8, 18:6, 20:4, OMAD (One Meal A Day), 5:2, and fully custom fasting schedules. You can adjust your eating and fasting windows to match your lifestyle and goals.

Does LeanFast track weight and body metrics?

Yes. LeanFast includes a weight tracker with trend charts so you can monitor your progress over time. Log your weight daily or weekly and see how your fasting routine correlates with your results.

How do I get started with intermittent fasting?

The easiest way to start is with the 16:8 plan: fast for 16 hours (including sleep) and eat within an 8-hour window. Download LeanFast, select the 16:8 plan, and the app will guide you with timers, reminders, and tips from the AI coach.
