Privacy Policy

Drawtopia — Learn to Draw · VertexLab Solutions LLC · Last updated September 11, 2026

The short version: your child's drawings never leave this device, and we never know who your family is.

There are no accounts, no sign-in, no ads, and no third-party tracking or advertising SDKs of any kind. From version 1.7, Drawtopia sends us two narrow things: anonymous daily counts (like how many lessons were finished) and any feedback your family chooses to send. Never drawings, names or photos.

What stays on your device

All of the following is stored only on the iPhone or iPad where Drawtopia is installed. We cannot see it, and nobody else can either:

  • Drawings — every finished lesson, free drawing, comic, flipbook and cake design in the gallery.
  • Artist profiles — the name your family types for each child, the picture they choose, and their age if you add it, so lessons can be pitched right.
  • Things your child writes — comic captions, names for invented creatures, the message on a cake design.
  • Progress — finished lessons, stars, stickers, the drawing streak and the skill map.
  • Settings — sound effects, music, reminders, the daily play limit and other parent choices.

Because none of this is copied anywhere, deleting the app deletes it permanently, and we have no way to recover it.

What leaves your device — only when you choose

  • Sharing a drawing. If you tap Share, the standard iOS share sheet sends the image wherever you pick — Messages, email, AirDrop and so on. It goes to that destination only; we never receive a copy.
  • Saving to Photos. Choosing Save Image asks for permission to add to your photo library. Drawtopia cannot read or browse your photos.
  • Printing. Printing a lesson or coloring pack uses AirPrint to send the page to your printer.

Anonymous usage totals

From version 1.7, each device sends a small daily tally so we can tell which lessons work and what to improve — for example how many lessons were finished, or how often the subscribe screen was seen that day. Each tally also says which app version and language you use, and whether the device is an iPhone or an iPad.

These counts carry no names, no identifiers and no drawings, and cannot be traced back to your child, your family or your device. They are totals for a day, nothing more.

Feedback your family chooses to send

  • Children can only answer in taps — a face rating for a lesson, or a vote for what to draw next. Children are never asked to type anything to us.
  • Parents can write to us from Parent Corner, behind the parent check. The screen shows exactly what will be sent before you send it. Please don't include personal details about your child.
  • An email address is optional, and only used to reply to you.

Written messages and reply addresses are encrypted on the device, so only we can read them.

Where that information is stored, and for how long

Usage totals and feedback go to our own private area of Apple's iCloud (CloudKit). No analytics or advertising company is involved at any point.

  • Anonymous daily totals and one-tap answers are deleted within 12 months.
  • Written messages from parents are deleted within 24 months, or sooner once resolved. Reply addresses are removed once we've replied.
  • To have feedback you sent deleted sooner, email us.

Reminders

If a parent turns on the daily drawing reminder, or the reminder before a free trial ends, the notification is scheduled by iOS on your device. There is no notification server and no push token is sent anywhere. Reminders can be switched off in Parent Corner or in iOS Settings.

Purchases

Drawtopia Pro is sold as an optional subscription through Apple. Apple handles all payment details — we never see your name, card or Apple Account. The app checks whether Pro is active using Apple's StoreKit on your device. Purchases sit behind a parent check, and Apple's Ask to Buy is supported. You can cancel at any time in iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions.

Children

Drawtopia is made for children aged about 4–10, and for the parents who set it up with them. We do not collect personal information from children. Anything a child enters — an artist name, a comic caption, a creature they invented — stays on the device and is never sent to us; children can only answer us in taps. Buying, opening links outside the app, changing parent settings and turning on reminders all require a parent check first.

Information Apple provides to developers

If you have opted in to share analytics with app developers in your device settings, Apple may give us aggregated, anonymous statistics such as download counts and crash reports. These never identify you or your child. Apple's handling of this is described in Apple's privacy policy.

Changes

If a future version of Drawtopia ever changes what the app collects, this page will be updated — with a new date — before that version is released.
