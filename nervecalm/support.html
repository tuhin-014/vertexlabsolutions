Subscriptions and billing

How do I cancel?

Subscriptions are billed by Apple, so cancelling happens in iOS Settings — not in NerveCalm:

  1. Open the iPhone Settings app
  2. Tap your name at the top, then Subscriptions
  3. Tap NerveCalm, then Cancel Subscription

Cancelling stops future charges. You keep Pro access until the end of the period you've already paid for, and nothing you've recorded is deleted — your check-ins, session history and progress stay on your device.

How do I avoid being charged after a free trial?

Cancel any time before the trial ends and you won't be charged. NerveCalm also sends you a reminder before a trial expires, so it shouldn't come as a surprise. Cancelling during a trial keeps your access until the trial's last day.

I already paid — how do I restore my purchase?

In the app: Settings → Restore Purchases. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple Account you bought with. If it still doesn't unlock, email us and include the Apple Account email you used.

I have a code from a friend

In the app: Settings → Redeem Code. If a code doesn't apply, email us the code and we'll check it.

Refunds

Refunds for App Store purchases are handled by Apple, not by us. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com. If something in the app didn't work as described, tell us too — we'd like to fix it.

What's free

NerveCalm works without an account and without paying. The free version includes:

  • Three breathing patterns — 4-7-8 Calm Breath, Extended Exhale, Box Breathing
  • Three somatic exercises — Somatic Shaking, Butterfly Hug, Orienting
  • The 60-second SOS reset and the daily check-in
  • Your streak, session history and the home-screen widget

Pro adds the full library — 17 somatic exercises, 7 breathing patterns, guided multi-day programs, the sound library, analytics and the therapist report.

Privacy

Nothing you do in NerveCalm leaves your phone.

There's no account, no sign-in and no server. Your check-ins, ratings, session history and any heart-rate readings are stored on your device only — we can't see them, and neither can anyone else. That's why the app works completely offline, on a plane or with no signal.

Because of this, we can't recover your history if you delete the app or lose your device. If you want a copy, use the therapist report export inside the app before you delete it.

Common questions

An exercise won't start, or the audio isn't playing

Check that your ringer isn't silenced and that Settings → Guided Voice is on inside the app. If it still doesn't work, email us and say which exercise, and which iPhone and iOS version you're on — that usually tells us straight away what's wrong.

Do I need headphones or a wearable?

No. Everything works with just your phone. Optional heart-rate readings use the iPhone camera, and if you wear an Apple Watch, NerveCalm can read your existing HRV from Apple Health — but nothing is required.

Can I use NerveCalm without sound?

Yes. Turn off Guided Voice in Settings and every exercise still works with on-screen guidance — useful at a desk, on a train or next to someone sleeping.

Why does the app ask how I feel before and after?

Those two ratings are the only way NerveCalm can learn which practices work for you. After a couple of rated sessions it starts showing which ones left you calmest. It's based entirely on your own ratings, and it stays on your device.

I'm not receiving reminders

Check iOS Settings → Notifications → NerveCalm is allowed, then re-check the reminder toggles in the app under Settings → Reminders.

If you're in crisis, please don't use an app.

In the US, call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline) or dial 911. Outside the US, contact your local emergency number.

NerveCalm is a wellness app. It isn't a medical device and doesn't diagnose, treat or prevent any condition. Nothing in it is medical advice — talk to a qualified professional about any health concern, and before starting a new practice if you have a medical condition.
