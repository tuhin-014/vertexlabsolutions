Before you start: keep it factual

  • Write what a camera would have seen. “Pickup scheduled 5:00 PM. Arrived 5:42 PM.” — not “he was being irresponsible again.”
  • Record it the same day. A contemporaneous note carries weight that a reconstruction written months later does not.
  • Don't characterize the other parent. A factual record helps you; an angry one can be handed back to you.
  • Note who else was there. A named witness turns your account into something that can be corroborated.
  • Keep receipts attached to the expense line they belong to, not in a separate pile.
  • Never alter an earlier entry. If you got something wrong, add a dated correction underneath and leave the original.
