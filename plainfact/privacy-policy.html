Plainfact — Custody & Kid Log

Last updated: July 21, 2026

Overview

Plainfact ("the App") is developed by VertexLab Solutions LLC. Plainfact is a personal record-keeping app for parents documenting custody matters. Our privacy design is simple: we hold nothing readable.

Data Collection

We collect nothing. Plainfact has no accounts, no analytics SDKs, no tracking, and no advertising. Your entries, photos, and documents are stored on your device. We cannot read your journal — nobody at VertexLab Solutions can.

Data Storage

All app data is stored locally on your device in an on-device database. No entry content is sent to any server, and we do not have access to it.

Purchases

Plainfact uses Apple's StoreKit for subscriptions. Purchase transactions are handled entirely by Apple and are subject to Apple's privacy policy. We receive no name, email, or payment details.

Your Data, Your Control

Plain export of your complete record is free, forever — regardless of subscription status. Deleting the App deletes your data from your device. We retain nothing on servers.

Children's Privacy

Plainfact is intended for adults. Information about children is entered by the parent and remains on the parent's device. We do not knowingly collect any information from children.

Legal Requests

Because records live only on your device, we have nothing to produce in response to legal requests beyond this policy and anonymous subscription records held by Apple.

Changes

We will post any changes to this policy on this page.
