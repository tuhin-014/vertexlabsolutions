Questions people actually ask

 

Does the other parent need to join?

No. Plainfact needs no invitation, no second account and no cooperation. It is built for one parent keeping their own record.

What does “sealed” actually mean?

Saving an entry records it into a chained, timestamped record and locks it. Later corrections are kept as visible edit history rather than replacing what you first wrote, and every binder ends with a certificate page showing the chain. It demonstrates an entry hasn't been altered inside Plainfact since sealing. It does not prove the events described occurred — whether any record is accepted is always decided by the court.

What happens to my records if I stop paying?

Nothing. Logging is free and unlimited, and exporting your complete record as a plain PDF or spreadsheet is free forever, subscription or not. The subscription pays for court-ready formatting: the binder, the certificate page, reimbursement requests and per-child summaries.

Is my data private?

Your entries and photos stay on your device. There are no accounts, no analytics SDKs, no tracking and no advertising, and the app makes no network requests carrying your content. Nobody here can read your journal. You can lock the app with Face ID, and its contents stay hidden in the app switcher.

Is this legal advice?

No. Plainfact is a personal record-keeping tool, not a law firm. It does not provide legal advice, is not a substitute for a licensed attorney, and does not guarantee any outcome. How records can be used depends on your state, your judge and your situation — decisions for you and your attorney.
