ClearScan — Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 27, 2026

ClearScan ("the App", "we", "our", "us") is a document scanning and PDF utility developed by VertexLab Solutions LLC. This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles your information.

1. Overview

ClearScan does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. All scanning, OCR, receipt reading, expiry-date detection, editing, signing, password protection, compression, merging, splitting, and photo-to-PDF conversion happen entirely on your device. We do not operate servers that receive your documents, and no data is sent to us or to any third party. Purchases of ClearScan Pro are handled by Apple (see section 7).

2. Data Stored on Your Device

The App stores the following data locally on your iPhone or iPad:

  • Scanned documents and PDF files you create or import
  • Document metadata (names, folders, creation dates, page counts)
  • Saved signatures you draw within the App
  • Details read from receipts you scan (store name, date, total, tax, category) and expiry dates found in documents
  • App preferences (default page size, default filter, alert and Home screen settings)
  • A short list of recent document names, shared with ClearScan's Home Screen widget on the same device (hidden when App Lock is on)

This data is stored in your device's app container and is never transmitted off-device unless you explicitly use iOS share-sheet actions (for example, sending a PDF via Mail, Messages, or AirDrop).

3. Permissions

ClearScan requests the following iOS permissions only when you first use the feature that needs them:

  • Camera — used to scan documents and read QR codes. Frames are processed on-device and never uploaded.
  • Photo Library — used only when you import photos to convert to PDF. Only the photos you pick are read, on-device.
  • Face ID — used only if you turn on App Lock, to unlock the App. Face ID is handled by iOS; the App never receives biometric data.
  • Notifications — used only for local reminders scheduled on your device: alerts before a scanned document (such as an ID or insurance card) expires, and a reminder before a free trial ends. No notifications are sent from a server, and no marketing notifications are sent.

You can change any of these permissions at any time in iOS Settings.

4. OCR (Optical Character Recognition)

Text recognition is performed entirely on-device using Apple's Vision framework. Scanned content is never sent to our servers or to any cloud OCR service.

5. No Account, No Sign-in

ClearScan does not require you to create an account or provide any personal information. There is no login, no email collection, and no user profile.

6. No Analytics or Tracking

The App does not include any analytics SDKs, advertising SDKs, crash-reporting services, or third-party trackers. We cannot see how you use the App.

7. In-App Purchases and Network Activity

ClearScan is free to download, with optional ClearScan Pro upgrades (a yearly subscription or a one-time Lifetime purchase). Purchases, subscriptions, renewals, and refunds are processed entirely by Apple through your Apple Account. We do not receive your name, email address, or payment details; the App only learns from Apple, on your device, whether you have an active Pro purchase.

Apart from communicating with Apple's App Store to make, verify, and restore purchases, ClearScan makes no network requests. Its document features work fully offline. Links to this policy and the Terms of Use open in Safari.

8. Children's Privacy

ClearScan does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13. Because we collect no data at all, the App is safe for use by people of any age.

9. Data Security

Because all data stays on your device, its security is governed by iOS itself. We recommend enabling a device passcode, Face ID, or Touch ID to protect your documents. PDFs you password-protect using ClearScan's "Protect" tool are encrypted using standard PDF encryption.

10. Data Retention and Deletion

Your documents remain on your device until you delete them from within the App, or until you uninstall the App (which removes all associated data).

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.

12. Contact

If you have questions about this policy, contact us at support@vertexlabsolutions.com.
