🔨 ContractorFlow — Privacy Policy

Last Updated: March 27, 2026

ContractorFlow ("we", "our", "us") is a business management platform for contractors and service professionals, operated by VertexLab Solutions. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly:

  • Account Information: Name, email address, phone number, business name, trade, address, and license number when you create an account.
  • Business Data: Jobs, invoices, estimates, expenses, mileage, customer records, photos, reviews, and schedule entries you create within the app.
  • Customer Data: Names, phone numbers, email addresses, and addresses of your customers that you add to the platform.
  • Payment Information: Billing details processed securely through Stripe. We do not store your credit card numbers.
  • Communications: SMS messages sent through our platform via Twilio for appointment confirmations, payment reminders, review requests, and missed call auto-replies.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and operate the ContractorFlow platform
  • To send transactional SMS messages (appointment confirmations, invoice reminders, review requests, missed call auto-replies)
  • To send transactional emails (invoices, estimates, payment reminders)
  • To process payments through Stripe
  • To improve our services and user experience
  • To respond to support requests and feedback

3. SMS/Text Messaging

ContractorFlow sends SMS messages on behalf of contractors to their customers for:

  • Missed call auto-replies
  • Appointment booking confirmations and reminders
  • Invoice payment reminders
  • Review requests after job completion

Consent: End users consent to receive messages when they: (1) provide their phone number while booking an appointment, (2) call or text the contractor's business number first, or (3) are added as a customer by the contractor with their consent.

Opt-Out: Recipients can opt out of SMS messages at any time by replying STOP to any message. Message and data rates may apply. Message frequency varies based on service usage.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with:

  • Supabase: Database hosting and authentication
  • Stripe: Payment processing
  • Twilio: SMS messaging services
  • Resend: Email delivery
  • Vercel: Application hosting

5. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including:

  • Row Level Security (RLS) on all database tables — users can only access their own data
  • Encrypted data transmission (HTTPS/TLS)
  • Secure authentication via Supabase Auth
  • PCI-compliant payment processing via Stripe

6. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. You can request deletion of your account and all associated data by contacting us at tuhin014@gmail.com.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Export your data (CSV export available in the app)
  • Opt out of marketing communications

8. Children's Privacy

ContractorFlow is not intended for use by children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any material changes by posting the new policy on this page.
