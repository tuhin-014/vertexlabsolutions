The Science

Why DopaFlow Works for ADHD

Traditional productivity tools are built for neurotypical brains. DopaFlow is not.

🔬 The ADHD Focus Problem

ADHD brains have lower baseline dopamine levels, which makes it harder to start tasks, maintain focus, and resist distractions. Standard timers ignore this entirely — they assume everyone can just "push through" a 25-minute block.

💡 How DopaFlow Solves It

  • Shorter, variable sprint lengths — match your actual attention capacity instead of forcing a fixed interval.
  • Immediate micro-rewards — each completed sprint delivers visual and haptic feedback that keeps your dopamine system active.
  • Low-friction task breakdown — turn a paralyzing task into a series of small, achievable steps you can sprint through.
  • Streak momentum — ADHD brains respond powerfully to streaks and visible progress. DopaFlow makes consistency feel rewarding, not exhausting.
  • No guilt, no shame — missed a session? DopaFlow encourages you to start fresh instead of showing you what you missed.

The result: you work with your ADHD brain instead of fighting against it.
